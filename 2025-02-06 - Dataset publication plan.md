## At a minimum:

### Basic Information:

- Treaty ID

- Treaty Name (full formal name

- Treaty Name (common name/abbreviation)

- Type (e.g., Bilateral, Multilateral)

- Date Adopted

- Date Opened for Signatures

- Date Entered into Force

  - Entry into force requirements

- Duration

  - Reservations allowed/prohibited

  - Sunset clauses

  - Withdrawal Provisions

  - Review Conference Schedule

  - Number of review conferences held

### Arms Control Specifics:

- Weapons/Systems Limited

- Type of Limitation (Use/Development/Stockpiling)

- Quantitative Limits (Zero/Specific Level)

- Behavior Change Required

- Verification Mechanisms

- Enforcement Mechanisms (Sanctions/What happens if caught)

- Third party roles specified

- Compliance History

### Participation & Context:

- Signatories & Dates

- Key Non-Signatories

- For US; party of President at the time

- Historical Background & Motivation

### Implementation Mechanics:

- Reporting requirements/deadlines

- Information exchange provisions

- National authority requirements

- Technical cooperation provisions

- Dispute resolution procedures

- Amendment procedures

- Extension provisions

### Others

- Treaty complexity (word count, number of articles)

- References to other treaties/international law
